## Overview
The site is the control board of a company that runs on software Doug built and operates alone. The signature object is the **system board**: every app is a row with a live status dot, an AI marker, and a 26-week strip of real weekly commit counts (`lib/activity.json`, generated by `scripts/build-activity.ts` from the local repos). Everything else borrows the board's grammar: labelled panels, mono measurements, one operational green. Headlines stay big and editorial so it never reads as a SaaS dashboard.

Scene: a hiring manager at an AI company, daylight office, laptop between tabs, often first on a phone. Light ground.

## Colors
Restrained world with one drenched moment. Paper ground, white panels, near-black ink. **Signal green** means "in production" and is the only accent: status dots, commit bars, links on hover, the primary metric. The **field** green is drenched once per page at most (the eval "referee" band, the closing contact block). Amber is only for honest "built, gated off / in progress" labels. Secondary text is `ink-2` (AA on paper and panel); `ink-3` only for non-essential meta at 12px+ on panel.

## Typography
Schibsted Grotesk for everything readable, heavy (800) at display size with tight tracking. Martian Mono only for measurements and machine text: commit counts, dates, domains, layer codes, figure numbers. Never mono for prose or headings.

## Layout
Max content width 1200px, 16px side gutter on phones, 32px from 768px. Sections separate by `spacing.section`, more space above a heading than below. The home first viewport is a two-column split from 1024px (thesis left, board right); below that the board stacks under the thesis and stays visible by the second scroll on phones.

## Elevation & Depth
Flat. Panels use a 1px `rule` border, no shadows, except screenshots, which sit in a frame with one soft offset shadow (`0 24px 48px -24px rgba(12,20,17,.35)`).

## Shapes
Panels 14px radius, controls 8px, status dots and bars square-ish (2px). No pills except tiny tags.

## Components
- **Board row:** status dot, app name, role (hidden under 640px), 26 bars, total in mono. Whole row is a link.
- **Screen frame:** thin top bar with live dot and domain in mono, then the screenshot. No fake traffic lights.
- **Screen viewer (home):** tabs per app across the frame top, caption and three feature lines alongside.
- **Tag:** mono 10-11px uppercase, 1px border. `AI` tag uses ink, gated uses amber.
- **Referee band:** field green, eval ladder drawn as a measured track from 60 to 100%.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do show real numbers from the repo data and PRODUCT.md evidence; label sandbox and anonymized material.
- Do keep one accent. Don't add violet, gold, gradients, glows, or noise textures.
- Don't put an eyebrow over every section. Allowed mono kickers: "Fig. n" on figures, the app's layer and role on an app page, and one green line on the eval band. Section headings are real h2s on a hairline (`SectionLabel`).
- Legacy Tailwind aliases (`muted`, `card`, `accent`, `border`, `background`, `foreground`) in `globals.css` map to the tokens above; prefer the real token names in new code.
- Don't use the old DK monogram.
- No em dashes, no native dialogs, reduced motion respected.
